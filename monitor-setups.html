Top Monitor Setups for Video Editing

Learn how to build a practical editing workstation around screen space, resolution and workflow.

By Saad · September 2026 · 7 min read

Why Monitor Setup Matters

Video editing involves timelines, preview windows, effects panels, audio controls and media libraries. More usable screen space can make these elements easier to manage.

Single Monitor Setup

A single large monitor can be a clean and simple solution. It keeps the desk organized while providing a focused editing workspace.

Dual Monitor Setup

A second display lets you separate your workflow. One screen can hold the timeline and controls while the other shows the preview or supporting applications.

Resolution

Higher resolution can provide more workspace and make detailed content easier to view. Your choice should also match your computer's graphics capability and the type of footage you edit.

Color Accuracy

Color consistency matters when editing video or creating graphics. Look for a display with suitable color reproduction for the work you actually do, and consider calibration when color accuracy is important.

Practical Desk Arrangement

  • Keep the main display directly in front of you.
  • Position a second screen at a comfortable angle.
  • Keep screens at a comfortable viewing height.
  • Leave enough space for the keyboard and mouse.
  • Manage cables to keep the workstation clean.

Final Thoughts

The best setup is the one that supports your workflow. A high-quality single display can be enough for some editors, while others will benefit from two screens.
